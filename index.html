    Experience

    My Work

    I started out making Kubernetes releases easier to build, test, and ship. That experience now shapes how I think about the day-to-day work of developers who use Kubernetes.

    1. Current

      Nutanix

      Member of Technical Staff III

      I focus on making it easier for developers to build with Kubernetes at Nutanix. I bring a release engineer’s perspective to the role, looking at where the path from code to a running workload gets harder than it needs to be.

    2. 2023–2026

      Broadcom

      R&D Software Engineer II

      My focus was shipping downstream Kubernetes distributions. I automated compatibility checks against upstream releases and maintained the pipelines that built secure container images.

    3. 2022–2023

      VMware

      Member of Technical Staff I & II

      I worked on Tanzu Kubernetes Grid releases. I automated builds and tests with Jenkins and Concourse and helped move repositories and CI workflows to GitHub Enterprise.
